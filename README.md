# Unitree

HTML and CSS product pages for Unitree Robotics quadruped robots, part of the Trossen Robotics web projects. Unitree builds commercial legged robots for research, education, and industrial use. These pages cover the Go1 family and the industrial B1.

## Products

| Product | Type | Description | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Go1 | Quadruped robot | Compact 12-DOF research and education quadruped weighing about 12 kg. Sold as Air, Pro, and EDU versions. EDU carries 5 kg (3 kg on the others), reaches 3.7 m/s, and climbs 35° slopes. | `Go1/` |
| Go1 Air | Quadruped robot | Entry-level Go1 version with a 2.5 m/s top speed. | `Go1 Air/` |
| B1 | Industrial quadruped | Weatherproof industrial quadruped with five depth cameras. Climbs stairs up to 20 cm and walks at about 1.8 m/s. | `Unitree B1/` |

## Structure

- `Go1/` - the Go1 product page and landing image
- `Go1 Air/` - the Go1 Air product page
- `Unitree B1/` - the B1 product page and styles

## Usage

No install or build step. Open any product's `.html` file in a browser to preview it. Keep the folder structure intact, or shared styles and images may not load.

## Notes

- Go1 specs follow Unitree's published datasheet. B1 figures vary between retailers, so check Unitree for current numbers.
- The Go2 followed the Go1. These pages cover the earlier generation.
